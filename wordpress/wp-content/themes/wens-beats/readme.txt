== Description ==

WENS Beats is a modern Full Site Editing (FSE) WordPress theme designed specifically for music-focused websites. Whether you're a solo artist, band, music label, DJ, producer, or event organizer, WENS Beats provides a flexible platform to showcase your music, events, releases, and creative work. Built with the latest WordPress block technology, the theme gives you complete control over your website’s layout and design without requiring coding skills. With a responsive, performance-focused structure, WENS Beats delivers a smooth experience across desktops, tablets, and mobile devices. The theme is also WooCommerce-compatible, making it easy to sell music, merchandise, tickets, and other products directly from your website. With its modern design, flexible customization options, and future-ready block-based architecture, WENS Beats gives music professionals everything they need to create a polished and engaging online presence.
